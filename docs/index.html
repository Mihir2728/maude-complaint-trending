What drove each signal month?

Excess reports over the baseline monthly mean, split by manufacturer, product problem, event type, report source and report origin (top 3 contributors each). One manufacturer or one problem taking most of the excess points to a specific issue (or a backlog submission) rather than a category-wide trend.

How to read this (MAUDE limitations)

  • MAUDE is a passive surveillance system. Counts reflect reporting, not incidence: without units sold or procedures performed there is no denominator, so a manufacturer with more reports may simply have more devices on the market.
  • Reports can be incomplete, unverified, duplicated or delayed, and a single submission can include backlogged events. A spike is a prompt to investigate, not evidence that a device caused harm.
  • Months are by date FDA received the report, not the event date, so reporting delays shift events later.
  • Manufacturer names are standardised with text rules and grouped by parent company (reference/manufacturer_aliases.csv). Literature/study reports are identified by keywords in the narrative, so some will be missed or mislabelled.
